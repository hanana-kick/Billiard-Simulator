prefix of the original accepted SMT2 descriptors when the initial contact cannot
fit the unchanged penetration/error budgets. It samples every body's full p/v/q
at that common time. Query budget failure, zero progress and ambiguous/unproved
intervals do not commit. It then caps the next local solve to64 Q20 ticks using
world header60; the cap is consumed by the next supported preparation.

physical enclosure. Original local budgets, contact penetration limits, immutable
shot deadline and component work accounting remain unchanged. A clear commit
consumes one world iteration and emits no collision event.
